Why Deals Fall Apart

Most service business sales don't fail because the business is bad. They fail because something comes up during the buyer's review that nobody prepared for.

Messy financials, too much depending on the owner, no clear plan for what happens after close. The buyer gets nervous, the price drops, or they walk. We've seen it from both sides. That's why we don't just run the sale — we make sure the business can hold up under a buyer's scrutiny before we bring anyone to the table.
